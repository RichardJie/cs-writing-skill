# Evidence and image-integrity checklist

## Data provenance

- Trace every panel to a source file, dataset accession, or documented analysis output.
- Record biological and technical replicate definitions.
- Confirm that denominators and excluded observations match the manuscript.
- Preserve raw data and make transformations reproducible.

## Statistical integrity

- Match the test to design, distributional assumptions, pairing, and repeated measures.
- Report effect size and uncertainty where possible.
- Declare multiplicity handling.
- Distinguish exploratory from confirmatory analyses.
- Avoid cut-points chosen on the same data without validation.

## Image integrity

- Do not selectively erase, duplicate, move, or generate experimental features.
- Apply brightness or contrast adjustments uniformly and disclose material processing.
- Preserve scale bars, aspect ratios, orientation, and crop context.
- Keep raw microscopy and gel/blot images unchanged and separately archived.
- Do not use illustration tools to repair or extend experimental images.

## Mechanistic integrity

- Do not equate co-expression, spatial proximity, ligand–receptor prediction, or enrichment with direct molecular interaction.
- Do not transfer causal conclusions across tumour types, species, or treatment contexts without explicit qualification.
- Mark negative, conflicting, and null evidence when it changes interpretation.
- Use causal arrows only when direction and intervention evidence support them.

## Privacy and ethics

- Remove direct identifiers and sensitive metadata from human images and source packages.
- Confirm consent and publication permission for recognizable clinical images.
- Do not upload protected health information to generative services.
