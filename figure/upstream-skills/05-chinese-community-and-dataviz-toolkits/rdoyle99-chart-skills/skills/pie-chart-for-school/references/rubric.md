# Typical rubric lines (check the assignment sheet first)

- Title says what the data is: "Favorite Sports in Class 6B, March 2026".
- Every slice is labeled with the category and the percent or angle.
- Slices are drawn to the right angle (within 2 degrees with a protractor).
- Slices add to 360 degrees and percents to 100.
- A key or direct labels are used.
- One sentence explains what the graph shows.
- Neat, with colors that can be told apart.
