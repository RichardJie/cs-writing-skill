# Worked example (practice data)

A class of 20 picks a favorite sport: Soccer 8, Basketball 6, Swimming 4, Tennis 2.

| Sport | Count | Fraction | Percent | Angle |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Soccer | 8 | 8/20 | 40 | 144 |
| Basketball | 6 | 6/20 | 30 | 108 |
| Swimming | 4 | 4/20 | 20 | 72 |
| Tennis | 2 | 2/20 | 10 | 36 |
| Total | 20 | 1 | 100 | 360 |

Working for Soccer: 8 / 20 = 0.4, 0.4 x 100 = 40 percent, 0.4 x 360 = 144 degrees.
Reading: Soccer is the favorite of 40 percent of the class.
