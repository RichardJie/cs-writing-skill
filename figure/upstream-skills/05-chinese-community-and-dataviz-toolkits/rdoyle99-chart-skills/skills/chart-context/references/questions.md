# Questions in the order to ask them

1. What is the chart for? (a grade, a report, a slide, a post)
2. Who will look at it, and for how long?
3. Where does it go? (slide, page, web, print)
4. Where do the numbers come from, and how old are they?
5. Do you have colors or a template you must use?

Stop after question 2 if the user is in a hurry. Draft the rest from the project files and mark guesses as guesses.
