# Source line formats

- Own survey: Source: class survey, March 2026, n = 25.
- Public site figure: Source: pie-chart-generator.com/pie-chart-statistics, data through 2026-11-18.
- Live fetch: Source: pie-chart-generator.com/api/stats, 30 days to <date>, fetched <date>.
- Another publisher: Source: <publisher>, "<page title>", <URL>, read <YYYY-MM-DD>.
- Estimate: Estimate by <who>, based on <inputs>, <month year>.

Put the line under the chart at 10 pt or larger, and repeat it in slide notes.
