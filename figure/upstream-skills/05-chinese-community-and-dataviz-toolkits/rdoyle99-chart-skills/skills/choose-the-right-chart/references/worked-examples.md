# Worked examples (illustrative data, made up for teaching)

## Monthly budget: rent 1,200, food 450, transport 150, fun 200
Four parts, one whole, the question is "where does the money go". Pie or doughnut. Biggest slice is rent at 57%, so label percentages.

## Quarterly sales by region over 8 quarters
Time is on the axis. Line, one series per region, or a grouped bar if there are only 2 quarters.

## Survey: 14 answer options, "pick one"
The parts add to 100% but there are 14. Sort a horizontal bar; or keep the top 5 as slices and group the rest as Other.
