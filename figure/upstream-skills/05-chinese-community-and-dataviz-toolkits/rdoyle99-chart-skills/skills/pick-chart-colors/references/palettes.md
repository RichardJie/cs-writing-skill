# Palettes (hex)

## Colorblind-safe categorical (Okabe and Ito, 2008)
Order: #0072B2 blue, #E69F00 orange, #009E73 green, #F0E442 yellow, #56B4E9 sky, #D55E00 vermilion, #CC79A7 pink, #000000 black.
Use the first 5 for most pies.

## One-hue sequential (light to dark, for ordered values)
#DEEBF7, #9ECAE1, #6BAED6, #3182BD, #08519C.

## Highlight one slice
Highlight #D55E00, everything else #BDBDBD, #D9D9D9, #EDEDED.

## Print in black and white
Pick fills at clearly different grays: #111111, #555555, #999999, #CCCCCC, and add direct labels.
