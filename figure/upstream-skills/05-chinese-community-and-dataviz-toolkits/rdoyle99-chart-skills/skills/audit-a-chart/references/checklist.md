# 12-point checklist

## Data
1. Parts add to a whole, or the chart is not a pie.
2. Percent labels total 100 (rounding up to 1 point is fine, note it).
3. No Total row charted as a slice.
4. Units are the same across values.

## Form
5. Not 3D.
6. 8 slices or fewer; Other is smaller than the largest named slice.
7. Bar charts start at zero.
8. Slices ordered largest to smallest, clockwise from the top (unless a natural order exists).

## Words
9. Title states the finding.
10. Unit and source with date present.
11. Labels are on the slices or beside them; no legend hunt.
12. Color is not the only separator (works in grayscale).
