# 05 — Action Grammar

For commercial / product / fashion video, "action" is a specific vocabulary. "She moves elegantly" tells the model nothing. This doc gives you 8 named action patterns used in beauty, fashion, and product advertising.

---

## Why this exists

Commercial directors choreograph human-product interactions with the same precision a fight choreographer choreographs combat. Each action has a recognizable beat structure.

If you can name the beat, the model can execute it.

---

## The 8 actions

### 1. Product reveal — the unveil

```
hand enters frame from camera-right at chest height,
fingers grip the cap with relaxed precision,
slow twist and lift over 1.5 seconds,
cap separates from base with subtle thread sound,
hand exits camera-up taking the cap with it,
base remains centered in frame
```

Beats: enter → grip → unveil → exit.

---

### 2. Product application — the wash / spray / apply

```
hand pumps the product into open palm,
palm closes briefly to warm and emulsify,
palm meets target surface (face / skin / hair),
slow spreading motion outward from center,
fingertips trail off in a graceful arc
```

Beats: pump → emulsify → contact → spread → trail-off.

---

### 3. Hair toss

```
subject starts with head tilted slightly down camera-right,
slow inhale lifts the shoulders,
head whips up and back over 0.8 seconds,
hair fans out in slow motion arc,
slight smile breaks at the apex of the toss
```

Beats: prep → inhale → whip → fan → emotion.

---

### 4. Walk toward camera

```
subject begins 8m from camera, weight on left leg,
first step forward initiates 3 seconds before camera reaches them,
arms swing naturally with steps,
eye line lifts toward camera at 2m distance,
subtle smile breaks 1m before frame fills,
final step plants directly in front of camera
```

Beats: start → walk → eye-up → smile → arrival.

---

### 5. Sip / drink / taste

```
hand brings glass to lips at relaxed pace,
glass meets lip with no spillage,
slow sip — visible swallow at throat,
glass lowers, eyes close briefly in satisfaction,
slight smile pulls at corner of mouth as glass settles
```

Beats: lift → contact → sip → swallow → react.

---

### 6. Look-up / reveal-eyes

```
subject begins with head down or eyes closed,
slow lift of chin over 1.2 seconds,
eyes open at peak — direct camera contact,
held gaze for 0.5 seconds,
slight smile breaks the stillness
```

Beats: down → lift → open → hold → break.

Classic beauty/perfume ad opener.

---

### 7. Hand pour / decant

```
bottle hovers above target surface at 30° tilt,
slow pour begins — liquid forms a clear strand,
strand catches the light, slight refraction,
liquid meets surface and spreads,
bottle returns to vertical, no drip
```

Beats: hover → tilt → pour → spread → return.

Critical for food / liquid / luxury ads. Light interaction with the liquid is the hero.

---

### 8. Apparel showcase — turn and pose

```
subject begins facing 45° away from camera,
slow rotation toward camera over 2 seconds,
weight shifts to forward leg as torso turns,
fabric of the garment follows the rotation with delay (drape physics),
final pose: hip cocked, weight back, gaze direct
```

Beats: start angled → rotate → fabric follows → land → gaze.

Drape physics is the realism marker — if the fabric snaps to the new position instantly, it looks fake.

---

## Action timing tables

Most commercial actions live in these durations:

| Action | Sweet-spot duration |
|---|---|
| Product reveal (cap, lid) | 1.5–2.5s |
| Application / spread | 2–4s |
| Hair toss | 0.8–1.2s |
| Walk to camera | 3–5s |
| Sip / drink | 2–3s |
| Look-up reveal | 1.5–2s |
| Pour | 2–3s |
| Turn pose | 2–3s |

If the model generates too fast: add "slow," "deliberate," explicit duration.
If too slow: add "snappy," "confident pace."

---

## Combining action + camera + lighting

A full commercial beat looks like:

```
[Action: hair toss, 1.2s duration]
[Camera: slow push-in 85mm shallow depth, 5s total]
[Lighting: backlit golden hour, hair rim glow during fan]
[Subject details: subsurface scattering, micro-expression at apex]
[Result: 5s shot where camera arrives at close-up just as hair toss peaks]
```

---

## Common mistakes

- ❌ "She moves" — give beats.
- ❌ Naming the product action without timing — model picks too fast.
- ❌ Forgetting the **emotional beat** — most commercial actions end on a small expression.
- ❌ Missing the **physics consequence** — if she tosses hair, hair must fan (don't forget the result of the action).
- ✅ Name the beats. Specify durations. Include the emotional landing.
