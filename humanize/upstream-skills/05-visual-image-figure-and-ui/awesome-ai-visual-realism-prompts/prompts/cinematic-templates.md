# Cinematic Templates (for video models)

For Seedance / Veo / Kling / Sora — video-specific prompts with motion, audio, and duration.

---

## Template 1 — Slow narrative push-in (5s)

```
[Seedance Narrative Mode]

A [character] [action] in [environment], [emotional state].

Camera: 35mm at f/2.0, shallow depth of field, eye-level. Slow steady
push-in from medium shot to medium close-up over 5 seconds. Smooth
gimbal, no shake. Subject remains centered throughout.

Subject realism: subsurface scattering on skin, catchlight in eyes,
subtle micro-expression building toward the end of the push.
Wardrobe: [fabric] with natural drape, slight motion as they breathe.

Lighting in the style of Deakins: single warm key from camera-[direction]
at [angle]°, negative fill on opposite side, motivated by [source].
Cool ambient with warm practical contrast.

Audio: room tone ambient, [specific 2-3 foley elements — fabric rustle,
soft breath, distant traffic], no music.

NEGATIVE: handheld shake, fast motion, multiple subjects, music,
plastic skin, deformed hands.
```

## Template 2 — Tracking sports / action (5-6s)

```
[Seedance Action Mode]

A [character] [action verb — running, jumping, sprinting, diving]
through [environment].

Camera: 28mm at f/2.8, deep DoF, chest height. Lateral tracking shot
parallel to subject's movement over 5 seconds. Intentional handheld
micro-shake. Subject stays centered as background rushes past.
Parallax depth between near and far elements.

Subject realism: athletic posture, weight forward in motion, muscle
tension visible, fabric flying with momentum (drape physics),
breath visible / sweat catching light.

Lighting: dramatic contrast — sun backlight rim glowing edges,
cool blue ambient shadows. Punchy teal-orange grade.

Audio: hard footsteps on [surface], heavy breath under exertion,
fabric movement, environmental impact sounds [splash / dirt / gravel].
No music.

NEGATIVE: slow motion drift, smooth gimbal, static camera, music,
t-pose, no shake.
```

## Template 3 — Product reveal hero (6s)

```
[Seedance Studio Mode]

A [product] sitting on [surface], soft directional studio light.

Camera: 85mm at f/4, slight low angle (5° below eye level), smooth
gimbal orbit around the product 180° over 6 seconds. Slow constant
speed, no shake. Product remains hero in frame throughout.

Material realism: [glass: Fresnel reflection, refraction through
contents / metal: anisotropic specular, mirror reflection of light /
ceramic: subtle glaze with light bloom]. Soft contact shadow under
base. Color bounce from product onto surface.

Lighting: clean studio key from camera-[direction] at 45° above,
softbox feel. Gentle fill from opposite side. Subtle rim light
camera-back for separation. Bright clean grade.

Audio: subtle product handling foley (cap touch, glass tap, fabric
brush), minimal ambient, no music.

NEGATIVE: handheld, fast motion, lens flare, color cast, busy
background, plastic-looking material, missing reflections.
```

## Template 4 — Atmospheric establishing (8s)

```
[Seedance Atmospheric Mode]

[Environment description — mountain valley at dawn / coastal cliffs
at dusk / dense forest after rain / urban skyline at night], no
human subject.

Camera: 24mm wide at f/8, deep DoF. Very slow crane up OR slow drift
forward over 8 seconds. Reveals the broader landscape.

Environmental realism: realistic atmospheric perspective (distant
objects haze blue), volumetric light if applicable (light beams
through trees, mist over water, dust in sunlight), realistic weather
[wind moving foliage / water flow / mist drifting].

Lighting: naturalistic, time-of-day appropriate. [Dawn: cool blue
shadows warming into orange highlights / Dusk: warm key, cool
ambient / Night: practical sources dominate, deep shadow elsewhere].

Audio: full environmental soundscape — wind, water, distant birds
or insects, occasional weather sound. No music.

NEGATIVE: artificial CGI look, fake lighting, missing atmospheric
haze, frozen vegetation, music, character appearance.
```

## Template 5 — Performance / dance / music (5-6s)

```
[Seedance Performance Mode]

A [performer] [action — dancing, playing instrument, singing] on
[stage / set], [single dominant lighting setup].

Camera: 85mm at f/2.0, eye level or slight low angle. Smooth steadicam
orbit around performer 90-180° over 6 seconds. Subject centered, stage
context rotating behind.

Subject realism: performance posture and dynamic motion, muscle tension
and release, costume movement following body (fabric drape physics),
sweat / catchlight on skin, micro-expressions of focus/joy.

Lighting: stage lighting respected. Single spotlight from above at
[angle]°, deep shadow elsewhere. Heavy diffusion, blooming highlights,
halation around the light source. High saturation color from gels.

Audio: performance-specific sounds — instrument [if applicable],
foot contact on stage, fabric movement, breath, distant audience
ambient. No backing music track.

NEGATIVE: static camera, full bright lighting, harsh shadows missing,
multiple lighting sources confusing, music track, frozen face.
```

## Template 6 — Walk to camera commercial (5s)

```
[Seedance Narrative Mode]

A [character] walks slowly toward camera in [environment],
[ending emotion — confident smile, curious gaze, recognition].

Camera: 50mm at f/2.0, eye level, slow push-in toward subject over
5 seconds. OR locked-off subject walking toward stationary camera
that catches them at the end.

Action beats:
0-1s: subject begins 6m from camera, first step.
1-3s: walking pace, arms swing naturally, eye line down or sideways.
3-4s: eye line lifts toward camera.
4-5s: arrives at medium close-up, subtle smile breaks, holds for
the final beat.

Subject realism: natural walk cycle with weight transfer, fabric drape
moving with each step, subsurface scattering on face, catchlights
in eyes, Duchenne smile activation in last beat.

Lighting: [golden hour backlight rim / soft overcast key / cinematic
key + negative fill] depending on mood.

Audio: footsteps on [surface], fabric movement, breath, ambient
environment sounds.

NEGATIVE: fast walk, mechanical movement, no eye contact, frozen
smile, t-pose at end, missing rim light.
```

## Audio cheat sheet (Seedance / Veo)

Always list 3-5 specific sound elements. Examples:

| Scene type | Foley elements |
|---|---|
| Indoor portrait | room tone, fabric rustle, soft breath, distant traffic |
| Outdoor walk | footsteps on [surface], wind, bird, distant sound |
| Product hero | cap click, glass tap, fabric brush, ambient hum |
| Sports / action | hard footsteps, heavy breath, fabric whip, impact sounds |
| Nature | wind, water, leaves, distant birds, insects |
| Performance | instrument, foot on stage, fabric, audience ambient |
| Food / drink | sizzling, liquid pour, glass tap, slice / scoop |
