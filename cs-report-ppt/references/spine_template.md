# 14-Slide Academic Spine Reference Guide (`Session Note Template` Standard)

This specification defines the authoritative **14-Slide Academic Presentation Spine** for transforming scientific papers (NeurIPS, ICML, CVPR, ICLR, KDD, ACL) into structured, conference-grade presentation decks.

Every deck generated by `academic-paper-presentation` strictly enforces this structural sequence to guarantee narrative clarity, rigorous critique, and actionable research translation.

## 2. Detailed Slide Specifications & Inspection Criteria

### Slide 1: Header / Title Slide
- **Section**: Header (1/1)
- **Tracker Badge**: `PAPER OVERVIEW • SESSION NOTE`
- **Slide Title**: Full academic paper title (concise, high impact, font dynamically scaled between 22pt and 28pt).
- **Layout Model**: `hero_1col` or dedicated title hero container.
- **Mandatory Elements**:
  1. **Primary Hero Card**:
     - Paper Title (Bold, 26pt).
     - Authors & Primary Affiliations (e.g., *Jane Doe, John Smith — Stanford University, DeepMind*).
     - Publication Venue, Year & Acceptance Status (e.g., *NeurIPS 2026 (Oral / Spotlight)*).
  2. **Metadata Badge Grid**:
     - **DOI / arXiv ID**: Active citation link (e.g., `arXiv:2405.04434 [cs.LG]`).
     - **Code Repository**: GitHub / Open-source artifact URL.
     - **Presenter**: Name, Laboratory / Group affiliation.
     - **Session Date**: Presentation date in ISO format (`YYYY-MM-DD`).
- **Narrative Role**: Establishes formal citation credentials and academic context.
- **Visual Inspection Gate**: Zero text truncation; author affiliations and metadata badges cleanly aligned without overlap.

---

### Slide 2: Motivation & Problem Context
- **Section**: Background (1/4)
- **Tracker Badge**: `BACKGROUND & MOTIVATION • SLIDE 02 OF 14`
- **Slide Title**: `Motivation & Real-World Discovery Context`
- **Subtitle**: `Current real-world applications face severe scalability and fidelity bottlenecks.`
- **Layout Model**: `split_65_35` (Main Card 65%, Research Sidenote 35%).
- **Mandatory Elements**:
  1. **Main Content Card (Left, 65%)**:
     - **Card Title**: `Domain Challenges & Failure Modes`
     - **Bullet 1 (Domain Urgency)**: Concrete real-world motivation driving this research.
     - **Bullet 2 (Existing Bottleneck)**: Why conventional paradigms (SOTA) fail under stress or scale.
     - **Bullet 3 (Core Opportunity)**: The key observation or theoretical opening exploited by authors.
  2. **Marginal Research Callout (Right, 35%)**:
     - **Badge**: `🔬 [State Memory Inertia]` or `[Model Adaptivity / Generalization]`
     - **Title**: `Relationship to Our Lab Trajectory`
     - **Content**: How this exact bottleneck manifests in our ongoing laboratory experiments.
- **Narrative Role**: Frames the problem as urgent and directly relevant to the audience.
- **Visual Inspection Gate**: Left card and right callout card maintain clear $0.30''$ gap; left vertical accent bar rendered sharply.

---

### Slide 3: Paper Classification & Literature Landscape
- **Section**: Background (2/4)
- **Tracker Badge**: `LITERATURE LANDSCAPE • SLIDE 03 OF 14`
- **Slide Title**: `Taxonomic Positioning & Literature Landscape`
- **Subtitle**: `Categorizing the methodological lineage and structural novelty within the field.`
- **Layout Model**: `grid_3col` (Three equal vertical cards).
- **Mandatory Elements**:
  1. **Card 1: Methodological Taxonomy**:
     - Theoretical vs Empirical categorization.
     - Optimization objective classification (e.g., variational inference vs contrastive learning).
  2. **Card 2: Architecture & Representation**:
     - Model topology (e.g., Sparse Mixture-of-Experts, State-Space Model, Multi-Head Latent Attention).
     - Parameter efficiency & compute bounds.
  3. **Card 3: Paradigmatic Positioning**:
     - Relative placement against dominant prior baselines.
     - Incremental refinement vs foundational paradigm shift.
- **Narrative Role**: Situates the paper within the broader scientific taxonomy.
- **Visual Inspection Gate**: 3 columns have identical widths ($3.877''$) and heights ($5.200''$); cards filled $\ge 75\%$ without dead space.

---

### Slide 4: Core Study Objective & Research Gap
- **Section**: Background (3/4)
- **Tracker Badge**: `RESEARCH OBJECTIVE • SLIDE 04 OF 14`
- **Slide Title**: `Central Study Objective & Identified Gaps`
- **Subtitle**: `Directly addressing the unverified assumptions and scalability boundaries of prior art.`
- **Layout Model**: `split_equal_2col` (Balanced 50/50 split).
- **Mandatory Elements**:
  1. **Card 1: Primary Research Question & Hypothesis**:
     - **Central Thesis**: The fundamental scientific hypothesis tested by the authors.
     - **Target Metric**: Primary mathematical invariant or empirical bound targeted.
     - **Scope of Validity**: Explicit domain boundaries where the hypothesis is asserted.
  2. **Card 2: Unresolved Research Gaps**:
     - **Gap 1 (Theoretical)**: Prior inability to bound error or guarantee convergence.
     - **Gap 2 (Computational)**: Prior memory bandwidth / FLOP quadratic scaling bottlenecks.
     - **Gap 3 (Empirical)**: Inconsistent generalization under out-of-domain distribution shifts.
- **Narrative Role**: Clarifies the precise gap the authors set out to close.
- **Visual Inspection Gate**: 50/50 cards symmetrical across vertical centerline ($X = 6.666''$); no bullet text overflow.

---

### Slide 5: Historical Evolution & Author Affiliations
- **Section**: Background (4/4)
- **Tracker Badge**: `HISTORICAL LINEAGE • SLIDE 05 OF 14`
- **Slide Title**: `Methodological Lineage & Research Trajectory`
- **Subtitle**: `Tracing architectural ancestry and institutional research continuity.`
- **Layout Model**: `split_65_35` (Lineage Evolution 65%, Lab Synergy 35%).
- **Mandatory Elements**:
  1. **Main Content Card (Left, 65%)**:
     - **Card Title**: `Evolutionary Milestone Timeline`
     - **Stage 1 (Origin, 2017-2020)**: Foundational formulation (e.g., Standard Multi-Head Attention).
     - **Stage 2 (Transition, 2021-2023)**: Intermediate optimizations (e.g., Multi-Query & Grouped-Query Attention).
     - **Stage 3 (Current Paper, 2024-2026)**: Proposed leap (e.g., Multi-Head Latent Attention with RoPE Decoupling).
     - **Author Lab Context**: Key previous publications from this specific research group.
  2. **Marginal Research Callout (Right, 35%)**:
     - **Badge**: `🔬 [Own Lab Synergy]`
     - **Title**: `Lineage Intersection with Our Codebase`
     - **Content**: Which version of the lineage our baseline builds upon and migration implications.
- **Narrative Role**: Explains how the field reached this point and author lineage credibility.
- **Visual Inspection Gate**: Chronological timeline milestones clearly delineated; lab synergy callout box formatted with theme accent tint.

### Slide 6: Inputs, Assumptions & Prerequisites
- **Section**: Methods (1/4)
- **Tracker Badge**: `METHODOLOGY: FOUNDATIONS • SLIDE 06 OF 14`
- **Slide Title**: `Input Representations & Foundational Assumptions`
- **Subtitle**: `Formal input spaces, data modalities, and operational preconditions.`
- **Layout Model**: `split_equal_2col` (50/50 split).
- **Mandatory Elements**:
  1. **Card 1: Input Modalities & Formal Spaces**:
     - Input tensor dimensionality (e.g., $\mathbf{x} \in \mathbb{R}^{B \times L \times D}$).
     - Tokenization, sampling rates, or feature normalization schemes.
     - Preprocessing pipelines and data augmentation constraints.
  2. **Card 2: Foundational Assumptions & Invariants**:
     - **Stationarity / Markovian Assumptions**: Explicit assumptions regarding data distribution.
     - **Lipschitz Continuity / Smoothness**: Regularity assumptions on loss or dynamics.
     - **Hardware Invariants**: Required GPU interconnects, cache hierarchy, or numerical precision (FP8/BF16).
- **Narrative Role**: Establishes prerequisite conditions before introducing complex equations.
- **Visual Inspection Gate**: Inline math variables formatted cleanly via DrawingML OMML; subscripts and superscripts legible.

---

### Slide 7: Mathematical Formulation & Objectives
- **Section**: Methods (2/4)
- **Tracker Badge**: `METHODOLOGY: FORMULATION • SLIDE 07 OF 14`
- **Slide Title**: `Formal Problem Formulation & Loss Objectives`
- **Subtitle**: `Analytical loss objectives and optimization constraints rendered in native OMML.`
- **Layout Model**: `split_equal_2col` or `asymmetric_2row`.
- **Mandatory Elements**:
  1. **Card 1: Loss Function & Optimization Objective**:
     - **Display OMML Equation**: Main mathematical loss (e.g., $\mathcal{L}_{\text{total}}(\boldsymbol{\theta}) = \frac{1}{N}\sum_{i=1}^N \ell(f(\mathbf{x}_i; \boldsymbol{\theta}), y_i) + \lambda \|\boldsymbol{\theta}\|_2^2$).
     - **Optimization Mechanism**: Gradient descent variant, Lagrange multipliers, or proximal operators.
  2. **Card 2: Mathematical Variable & Operator Glossary**:
     - Variable definition table: symbol $\rightarrow$ dimensionality $\rightarrow$ physical / geometric meaning.
     - Boundary constraints and projection operators ($\Pi_{\Omega}$).
- **Narrative Role**: Provides rigorous, reproducible mathematical grounding.
- **Visual Inspection Gate**: Display OMML formulas centered with proper operator sizing; zero raw LaTeX markup leaks (`$$` or `\frac`).

### Slide 8: Technical Mechanism & Architecture
- **Section**: Methods (3/4)
- **Tracker Badge**: `METHODOLOGY: ARCHITECTURE • SLIDE 08 OF 14`
- **Slide Title**: `Architectural Mechanism & Algorithmic Pipeline`
- **Subtitle**: `End-to-end tensor transformations and component interaction mechanisms.`
- **Layout Model**: `split_65_35` (High-DPI Architecture Figure 65%, Algorithmic Steps & Callout 35%).
- **Mandatory Elements**:
  1. **Main Card (Left, 65%)**:
     - **High-DPI Architecture Diagram**: Extracted from PDF (300+ DPI) with exact aspect ratio, or native DrawingML vector flowchart.
     - **Data Flow Highlights**: Annotations indicating key tensor routing paths.
  2. **Side Card (Right, 35%)**:
     - **Algorithmic Step Breakdown**: Sequential 3-step execution flow.
     - **Marginal Research Callout**:
       - **Badge**: `🔬 [Mechanistic Interpretability]`
       - **Connection**: Internal representation geometry and attention head routing relevance.
- **Narrative Role**: Connects theoretical formulation with concrete architecture execution.
- **Visual Inspection Gate**: Architecture figure crisp and un-distorted (aspect ratio preserved); step numbers and callout text aligned.

### Slide 9: Experimental Design & Modalities
- **Section**: Methods (4/4)
- **Tracker Badge**: `EXPERIMENTAL PROTOCOL • SLIDE 09 OF 14`
- **Slide Title**: `Experimental Protocol, Benchmarks & Baselines`
- **Subtitle**: `Evaluation benchmarks, comparative baselines, and compute infrastructure.`
- **Layout Model**: `grid_3col` (3 equal vertical cards).
- **Mandatory Elements**:
  1. **Card 1: Benchmark Datasets & Modalities**:
     - Benchmark suite names (e.g., ImageNet-1K, MMLU, GSM8K, HumanEval).
     - Data volume, split ratios, and evaluation shot counts (0-shot, 5-shot).
  2. **Card 2: Competitive Baseline Classes**:
     - Dominant standard baselines (e.g., LLaMA-3, Mistral, Dense Transformers).
     - Specialized SOTA methods matching parameter scale.
  3. **Card 3: Training & Compute Budget**:
     - Hardware cluster (e.g., 64x NVIDIA H100 SXM5 GPUs).
     - Optimizer settings (AdamW, $\beta_1=0.9, \beta_2=0.95$, cosine decay schedule).
- **Narrative Role**: Validates empirical rigor before examining performance numbers.
- **Visual Inspection Gate**: 3 vertical cards evenly spaced; parameter badges styled with clear visual contrast.

---

### Slide 10: Primary Quantitative Benchmarks
- **Section**: Results (1/3)
- **Tracker Badge**: `BENCHMARK RESULTS • SLIDE 10 OF 14`
- **Slide Title**: `Primary Benchmark Performance & SOTA Comparison`
- **Subtitle**: `Consistently outperforming competitive baselines across standard benchmark suites.`
- **Layout Model**: `asymmetric_2row` (Master Table Top, 3 Takeaway Cards Bottom) or `split_equal_2col`.
- **Mandatory Elements**:
  1. **Primary Results Table Card (Top / Left)**:
     - Structured multi-column benchmark table or harvested high-DPI paper table.
     - Bold formatting for top-performing metrics (SOTA).
     - Explicit percentage / absolute improvement deltas ($\Delta +4.8\%$).
  2. **Statistical Significance & Takeaways (Bottom / Right)**:
     - Error bars, confidence intervals ($p < 0.01$), or multi-seed standard deviations.
     - 3 concise quantitative takeaway badges.
- **Narrative Role**: Establishes empirical superiority with clean statistical evidence.
- **Visual Inspection Gate**: Table headers distinct; best numbers bolded; improvement deltas highlighted with accent color.

---

### Slide 11: Baseline Categories & Trade-off Analysis
- **Section**: Results (2/3)
- **Tracker Badge**: `COMPARATIVE ANALYSIS • SLIDE 11 OF 14`
- **Slide Title**: `Paradigm-Wise Comparisons & Pareto Trade-offs`
- **Subtitle**: `Balancing accuracy gains against computational throughput and memory footprints.`
- **Layout Model**: `split_65_35` (Trade-off Analysis 65%, Efficiency Callout 35%).
- **Mandatory Elements**:
  1. **Main Card (Left, 65%)**:
     - **Paradigm Comparison**: Breakdown across different structural paradigms.
     - **Pareto Trade-off Curve**: Throughput (tokens/sec) vs Accuracy (MMLU score) or FLOPs vs mAP.
     - **Operating Regime**: Identification of regimes where proposed method dominates vs degrades.
  2. **Marginal Research Callout (Right, 35%)**:
     - **Badge**: `🔬 [Computational & Hardware Efficiency]`
     - **Relevance**: Memory footprint and execution speed on our target edge / datacenter hardware.
- **Narrative Role**: Provides honest trade-off analysis beyond simple leaderboard rankings.
- **Visual Inspection Gate**: Trade-off curve / asset aspect locked; callout card specifies concrete VRAM/latency implications.

---

### Slide 12: Ablation Studies & Sensitivity Analysis
- **Section**: Results (3/3)
- **Tracker Badge**: `ABLATION & SENSITIVITY • SLIDE 12 OF 14`
- **Slide Title**: `Component-Wise Ablations & Sensitivity Dynamics`
- **Subtitle**: `Dissecting the empirical contribution of individual architectural components.`
- **Layout Model**: `quadrant_2x2` (4 quadrant cards) or `split_equal_2col`.
- **Mandatory Elements**:
  1. **Quadrant 1: Component Contribution Ablation**:
     - Incremental performance drop when removing modules ($-\Delta 3.2\%$ without RoPE decoupling).
  2. **Quadrant 2: Hyperparameter Sensitivity**:
     - Robustness across latent dimension sizes ($d_c \in \{64, 128, 256\}$).
  3. **Quadrant 3: Scaling Curves**:
     - Compute / token scaling efficiency ($N^{\alpha}$ power laws).
  4. **Quadrant 4: Failure Mode Analysis**:
     - Edge cases where performance gains plateau or saturate.
- **Narrative Role**: Proves that gains stem from intended innovations, not superficial tuning.
- **Visual Inspection Gate**: 4 quadrant cards have uniform horizontal/vertical gaps ($G_x = 0.30'', G_y = 0.25''$); headers aligned.

---

### Slide 13: Author Conclusions vs Critical Limitations
- **Section**: Conclusion (1/2)
- **Tracker Badge**: `CRITICAL ASSESSMENT • SLIDE 13 OF 14`
- **Slide Title**: `Author Contributions vs Independent Critical Assessment`
- **Subtitle**: `Contrasting claimed breakthroughs against unaddressed theoretical and empirical boundaries.`
- **Layout Model**: `split_equal_2col` (50/50 Balanced Critique Split).
- **Mandatory Elements**:
  1. **Card 1: Author-Claimed Breakthroughs**:
     - Core architectural and empirical contributions claimed by the original authors.
     - Validated theoretical guarantees and primary benchmark conquests.
  2. **Card 2: Independent Critical Limitations & Blindspots**:
     - **Omitted Baselines**: High-performing concurrent baselines not included in comparison.
     - **Hidden Compute Costs**: Unreported pre-training overhead or fine-tuning instabilities.
     - **Generalization Ceiling**: Unverified claims on extreme out-of-domain data.
- **Narrative Role**: Applies the highest standard of academic scrutiny (preventing defensive cheerleading).
- **Visual Inspection Gate**: Balanced 50/50 card split; both positive achievements and critical limitations given equal visual weight.

---

### Slide 14: Seminar Discussion Questions & Actionable Lab Roadmap
- **Section**: Conclusion (2/2)
- **Tracker Badge**: `SEMINAR DISCUSSION • SLIDE 14 OF 14`
- **Slide Title**: `Seminar Discussion Prompts & Laboratory Roadmap`
- **Subtitle**: `Forward-looking research inquiries and immediate actionable translation milestones.`
- **Layout Model**: `split_65_35` (Discussion Prompts 65%, Lab Roadmap 35%) or `grid_4col`.
- **Mandatory Elements**:
  1. **Main Card (Left, 65%)**:
     - **Prompt 1 (Theoretical Extension)**: Can the latent compression guarantee preserve non-linear invariant kernels?
     - **Prompt 2 (Cross-Domain Transfer)**: How does this mechanism transfer from NLP to continuous robotics control?
     - **Prompt 3 (Scale Boundary)**: What breaks when scaling from 16B to 500B active parameters?
  2. **Marginal Research Action Card (Right, 35%)**:
     - **Badge**: `🔬 [Own Lab Synergy]` / `[Actionable Lab Roadmap]`
     - **Milestone 1 (Immediate, 1-2 Weeks)**: Implement custom PyTorch module for baseline benchmarking.
     - **Milestone 2 (Medium-term, 1 Month)**: Integrate latent KV cache compression into our agent framework.
     - **Milestone 3 (Hypothesis Test)**: Verify whether latency reductions translate to real-time interactive tasks.
- **Narrative Role**: Transforms passive presentation listening into active collaborative research.
- **Visual Inspection Gate**: 3 seminar questions clear and thought-provoking; 3-stage roadmap items concrete and time-bounded.
